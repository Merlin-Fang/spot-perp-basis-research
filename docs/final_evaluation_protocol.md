For event indicator E=1{observed T<=h}, survivor indicator A=1{followup>=h and no earlier event}, probability p and fitting censor survival G,

\[
BS(h)=\frac1n\sum_t\left[\frac{E_t(1-p_t)^2}{G(T_t-1)}+\frac{A_t p_t^2}{G(h)}\right].
\]

These components do not implement the annual final pipeline. In particular, `data/windows.py` still protects pre-2023 data, `fit_hourly` and calibration coordinators remain development-only, `fit_survival` relies on caller-supplied origin/purge restrictions, the financial block helper defaults to a 2022 anchor, and replay has no restart API. Keep those protections; build explicit versioned adapters later. `probability_gate` alone does not validate a global KM reference. Scoring paired-difference/interval support must be made consistent with the canonical metric, not inherited from old clipping helpers.

Remaining gates are: (1) parameterized annual fitting/calibration; (2) causal feature/publication cutoffs; (3) strict 16h purge and lower origin bounds; (4) matched risk sets; (5) annual static KM/maps and causal rolling KM; (6) common IPCW support; (7) unavailable-model/reference handling; (8) continuous cross-year financial state; (9) pending orders and consumed-event preservation; (10) leap-2024 accounting; (11) terminal marking and unknown-cash detection; (12) portable source-parity, future-mutation and leakage tests; (13) explicit holdout-access authorization; (14) DOGE/ADA and EFFR data preparation plus unified final-period QA.

First implement bounded rolling-window/model-fit adapters using synthetic and explicitly permitted development-only data, with no final input access or automatic execution. Preflight must establish support without changing registered windows/gates. Acquisition, final predictions/accounting and performance release are subsequent separately authorized stages. **Scientific freeze is complete; engineering readiness and final-data authorization are not.**
